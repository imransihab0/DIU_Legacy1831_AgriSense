## How FRG doses are adjusted
- FRG recommendations are keyed to a **soil-test fertility class**: Optimum,
  Medium, Low, Very low (p.86). Lower soil fertility → higher dose.
- The values in this file are the **Medium** class (typical default when no
  soil test is available); the guide gives higher ranges for Low/Very low soils.
- Nutrient doses are **kg/ha of the element** (N, P, K, S, Zn, B), plus an
  Organic Fertilizer (OF) rate in t/ha where listed.
- Yield Goal (t/ha) is stated per crop/variety; doses target that yield.
- Conversion to product bags depends on fertilizer grade (see note above).

## Nutrient roles quick reference
- N (Nitrogen): vegetative growth, leaf/tiller development, protein.
- P (Phosphorus): root establishment, early vigour, grain/tuber set.
- K (Potassium): water regulation, stalk strength, disease/lodging resistance.
- S (Sulphur): oilseed quality, protein; deficiency common in BD soils.
- Zn (Zinc): critical for rice; deficiency causes stunting/hopperburn-like.
- B (Boron): flowering, grain/fruit set; key for mustard, vegetables.

## Boro rice — fertilizer (p.86)
- Var group (BRRI dhan29/58/89/92/96, Bangabandhu dhan100, BRRI dhan102, Binadhan-14/18/24/25). Yield Goal 7.5 ± 0.75 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 59–116, P 7–12, K 41–80, S 7–12, Zn 0.0–1.3. OF 2 t/ha (Optimum row).
- Second Boro var group (BRRI dhan28/50/63/67/81/84/86/88, Binadhan-8/10): Yield Goal 6.0 ± 0.6 t/ha. Medium: N 47–92, P 6–10, K 33–64, S 6–10, Zn 0–1.0.
- Application (p.86): all P, K, S, Zn basal at final land prep. Light soils: half K basal, half with last N topdress. N in **3 equal splits** — basal/after establishment, early tillering, and 5–7 days before panicle initiation.
- USG note (p.87): ~30% urea-N saved with USG; if N=150 kg/ha, apply 110 kg N/ha as USG + 40 kg N/ha topdress before PI.

## Aman rice — fertilizer (p.87)
- T. Aman var group (BRRI dhan72/73/87/93/94/95/103, Binadhan-16/17/22/23). Yield Goal 6.0 ± 0.6 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 31–60, P 6–10, K 28–54, S 5–8, Zn 0–0.9.
- Cropping-pattern reference dose for T. Aman (p.178): N 90, P 6, K 35–43, S 6–7, Zn 1 (kg/ha).

## Aus rice — fertilizer (p.87)
- Var group (BRRI dhan48/82/85/98/106, Binadhan-21). Yield Goal 5.0 ± 0.5 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 25–48, P 5–8, K 23–44, S 4–6, Zn 0.0–0.7.
- Second Aus var group (BRRI dhan27/83, Binadhan-19): Yield Goal 4.0 ± 0.4 t/ha. Medium: N 22–42, P 4–6, K 20–38, S 2.6–5.0, Zn 0.0–0.5.

## Wheat — fertilizer (p.178, cropping-pattern table)
- FRG gives no single standalone wheat table; doses appear in cropping patterns. Yield Goal 4.0 ± 0.4 t/ha.
- Representative Rabi wheat dose, kg/ha: N 120, P 20–30, K 60–90, S 15, Mg 4–8, Zn 1.5–3, B 2 (p.175/178/180).

## Maize (Bhutta) — fertilizer (p.90)
- Var (BARI Hybrid Bhutta-9/13/14/15/16/17, BWMRI Hybrid Maize-1/2). Yield Goal 11.0 ± 1.1 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 81–160, P 19–36, K 46–90, S 13–24, Mg 6–10, Zn 0.0–2.0, B 0.0–1.0. OF 3 t/ha.
- Application (p.90) Rabi: 1/3 N + all P,K,S,Zn,B + OF in furrows at sowing; half of remaining N at 50–55 DAS, rest at tasselling (80–85 DAS). **Kharif: reduce dose by 30%.**

## Potato (Aloo) — fertilizer (p.105)
- Many BARI Alu varieties (Alu-7 Diamant … Alu-91). Yield Goal 30.0 ± 3.0 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 46–90, P 11–20, K 46–90, S 6–10, Mg 0–5, Zn 0–2.0, B 0–0.8. OF 3 t/ha.
- Application (p.105): all OF, P, K, S, Mg, Zn, B + half of N and K basal at final land prep; remaining half N and K as side dressing at 30–35 DAP during earthing up.

## Mustard (Sarisha) — fertilizer (p.101)
- Var (Tori-7, BARI Sharisa-14/15/20, Safal, Agrani, Binasharisa-6/7/8). Yield Goal 1.8 ± 0.18 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 31–60, P 9–16, K 21–40, S 6–10, Mg 0.0–2.5, Zn 0.0–1.0, B 0.0–0.5. OF 3 t/ha.
- S and B matter for oilseed yield; note the explicit B recommendation.

## Lentil (Masur) — fertilizer (p.175/178, cropping-pattern)
- No standalone lentil table; appears in Sugarcane+Lentil year-round pattern. Yield Goal 1.8 ± 0.18 t/ha.
- Legume note: like other pulses (see Chickpea p.98), **Rhizobium inoculum (~1.5 kg/ha) can replace N fertilizer** if available — a cost saver.

## Onion (Piaj) — fertilizer (p.122, Summer Onion)
- Var (BARI Peyaz-2/3/5). Yield Goal 12.0 ± 1.2 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 26–50, P 11–20, K 21–40, S 9–16, Zn 0–1.0, B 0.0–0.5. OF 3 t/ha.
- Application (p.122): all OF, P, S, Zn, B + half N and K basal; remaining N and K in 2 splits at 25 and 50 DAP (bulb) or 3 splits at 25/50/75 DAP (seed).

## Tomato — fertilizer (p.112–113)
- Winter (BARI Tomato-11…21, Binatomato-5…12): Yield Goal 75.0 ± 7.5 t/ha. Medium, kg/ha: N 41–80, P 16–30, K 26–50, S 8–14, Mg 5–8, Zn 0–1.6, B 0–1.0. OF 5 t/ha.
- Summer (BARI Hybrid Tomato-4/8/10/11, Binatomato-2/3): Yield Goal 45.0 ± 4.5 t/ha. Medium, kg/ha: N 31–60, P 12–22, K 26–50, S 8–14, Zn 0–1.5, B 0–1.0. OF 4 t/ha.
- Application (p.113): half OF + all P,S,Zn,B basal; N and K in 3 equal splits at 20/40/60 DAT (ring method).

## Brinjal (Begun) — fertilizer (p.113)
- Var (BARI Begun-4…12, BARI Hybrid Begun, BARI Bt Begun-1…4). Yield Goal 50.0 ± 5.0 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 46–90, P 21–30, K 41–80, S 6–10, Mg 5–8, Zn 0–1.5, B 0–1.0. OF 7 t/ha.

## Jute (Pat) — fertilizer (p.93)
- Tossa jute (O-4, O-9897, BJRI Tossa Pat 4–9). Yield Goal 4.4 ± 0.44 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 36–70, P 7–12, K 26–50, S 7–12, Zn 0.0–2.0.
- Cropping-pattern jute (O) reference (p.178): N 105, P 7, K 33–40, S 9–11, Zn 1.5–2 (kg/ha).

## Chickpea (Chola) — fertilizer (p.98)
- Var (BARI Chola-5…11, Binasola-6…10). Yield Goal 2.0 ± 0.2 t/ha.
- Medium soil, kg/ha: N 10–18, P 7–12, K 9–16, S 5–8, Zn 0.0–1.0, B 0.0–0.6, Mo 0–0.4.
- Application (p.98): all basal at final land prep; **Rhizobium inoculum 50 g/kg seed or 1.5 kg/ha — if used, apply NO N fertilizer.**
