# Crop Calendar and Season Guide — Bangladesh
Source: Compiled from DAE (Department of Agricultural Extension) Khamari pocket guides and BRRI (Bangladesh Rice Research Institute) rice production guides — dae.gov.bd, brri.gov.bd.

## Seasons
- Rabi (winter, dry): mid-October to mid-March. Crops: boro rice (seedbed Nov-Dec, transplant Dec 15-Jan 31), wheat (sow Nov 15-Dec 15; after Dec 20 yield drops ~1.3% per day of delay), maize (Nov-Dec), potato (Nov 10-Dec 10), mustard (Oct 15-Nov 15), lentil (Oct 20-Nov 20), onion (transplant Nov-Dec 15), winter tomato (transplant Oct-Nov 15).
- Kharif-1 (pre-monsoon): mid-March to mid-July. Crops: jute (sow Mar 15-Apr 30), aus rice, summer vegetables, mungbean.
- Kharif-2 (monsoon): mid-July to mid-October. Crops: transplanted aman rice (Jun 15-Aug 15; optimum mid-July), late jute harvest.

## Boro rice timeline (transplanting day = T)
- T-45 to T-30: seedbed preparation and sowing.
- T-7: final land preparation (2-3 ploughings, puddling), basal fertilizer (TSP, MoP, gypsum, zinc).
- T: transplant 35-45 day old seedlings, 2-3 per hill, 20x20 cm spacing.
- T+15: first urea split; maintain 2-5 cm standing water.
- T+30/35: second urea split; first hand weeding.
- T+50/55: third urea split before panicle initiation; critical irrigation period — never let field dry from panicle initiation to grain filling.
- T+70 to T+95: flowering and grain filling; monitor for blast and BPH.
- T+110 to T+125: drain field 10-12 days before harvest; harvest at 80% golden grain.

## Wheat timeline (sowing day = S)
- S-10: land preparation, basal fertilizer.
- S: sow 48 kg seed/acre in lines 20 cm apart.
- S+18 (CRI stage): top-dress remaining urea, then first irrigation — the single most yield-critical event for wheat.
- S+35: weeding.
- S+55 (booting): second irrigation.
- S+75 (grain filling): third irrigation if no rain.
- S+105 to S+115: harvest when spikes turn golden and grain is hard.

## Potato timeline (planting day = P)
- P-10: land preparation with cowdung; basal fertilizer.
- P: plant 600 kg/acre certified seed tubers, 60x25 cm.
- P+30/35: earthing-up + remaining urea and MoP; light irrigation after.
- P+40 onward: late blight watch — preventive fungicide (mancozeb) every 7-10 days in cool foggy weather (Dec-Jan).
- P+80: stop irrigation.
- P+90/95: haulm cutting, harvest 10 days later; cure in shade before storage.

## Weather rules of thumb
- Transplanting or sowing right before forecast rain of >30 mm in 3 days: delay and let field drain — seedling submergence and seed rot risk.
- Urea top-dress: postpone if >20 mm rain is forecast within 3 days.
- Potato/tomato in foggy, humid weeks (RH >85%, t_min <14°C): late blight pressure is severe — tighten fungicide interval.
- Heat stress: boro rice flowering above 35°C causes spikelet sterility; wheat grain filling above 30°C shrinks grain.
