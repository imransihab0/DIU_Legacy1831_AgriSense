# Fertilizer Recommendations for Major Crops of Bangladesh
Source: Compiled from BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide (FRG-2018), Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council — publicly available at barc.gov.bd.

## Boro Rice (irrigated, HYV)
Per acre: Urea 110 kg, TSP 40 kg, MoP 48 kg, Gypsum 24 kg, Zinc sulphate 4 kg.
Urea is applied in 3 equal splits: (1) 15 days after transplanting, (2) at maximum tillering (30-35 DAT), (3) 5-7 days before panicle initiation (50-55 DAT). Do NOT apply urea immediately before heavy rain — nitrogen washes away as runoff. TSP, MoP, gypsum and zinc are applied fully at final land preparation. On sandy soils, split MoP into two applications because potassium leaches. Zinc deficiency is common in continuously flooded rice; symptoms are brown blotches on young leaves.

## Aman Rice (transplanted, rainfed)
Per acre: Urea 75 kg, TSP 32 kg, MoP 32 kg, Gypsum 20 kg.
Urea in 3 splits at 10-15 DAT, 25-30 DAT and before panicle initiation. In flood-prone areas delay the first split until water recedes.

## Wheat
Per acre: Urea 88 kg, TSP 54 kg, MoP 32 kg, Gypsum 44 kg, Boron 3 kg.
Two-thirds of urea plus all other fertilizers at final land preparation; remaining one-third urea at crown root initiation stage (17-21 days after sowing) followed immediately by first irrigation. Wheat needs 2-3 irrigations: CRI stage (critical), booting, and grain filling.

## Maize (hybrid, rabi)
Per acre: Urea 110 kg, TSP 60 kg, MoP 55 kg, Gypsum 45 kg, Zinc sulphate 4 kg, Boron 2.5 kg.
One-third urea basal; one-third at 8-leaf stage (30-35 DAS); one-third at tasseling (60-65 DAS). Irrigate at 4-leaf, 8-leaf, tasseling and grain-filling stages.

## Potato
Per acre: Urea 100 kg, TSP 60 kg, MoP 100 kg, Gypsum 40 kg, Zinc sulphate 4 kg, Magnesium sulphate 16 kg, plus 4 tons decomposed cowdung.
Half urea and half MoP at planting; remaining half of both at earthing-up (30-35 days after planting). Potato is potassium-hungry; under-application of MoP reduces tuber size. Stop irrigation 10 days before harvest.

## Mustard
Per acre: Urea 100 kg, TSP 68 kg, MoP 34 kg, Gypsum 60 kg, Boron 3.5 kg.
Half urea plus all other fertilizers basal; half urea at flower initiation (25-30 DAS) with irrigation. Sulphur (gypsum) is critical for oilseed yield.

## Lentil
Per acre: Urea 18 kg, TSP 34 kg, MoP 14 kg, Boron 3 kg.
All basal. As a legume, lentil fixes its own nitrogen; excess urea reduces nodulation and encourages fungal disease.

## Onion
Per acre: Urea 96 kg, TSP 72 kg, MoP 60 kg, Gypsum 44 kg, plus 3 tons cowdung.
One-third urea basal; remainder in two splits at 25 and 50 days after transplanting.

## General soil guidance
- Sandy / sandy loam soils: lower water-holding capacity — apply nitrogen and potassium in more, smaller splits; irrigate lightly and more frequently.
- Clay soils: hold water and nutrients longer — fewer, heavier irrigations; drainage matters more than irrigation in monsoon.
- Soil pH below 5.5: apply dolomite lime 400 kg/acre once every 3 years, ideally 1 month before planting.
- Never broadcast urea onto standing water right before rainfall of more than 20 mm — expected loss 30-50% of applied N.
