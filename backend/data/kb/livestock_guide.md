# Livestock rearing, feeding & health guide (Bangladesh)

Indicative standard practice from DLS/BLRI extension guidance. Vaccination timing and
doses vary by product and locality — confirm with a local vet or Upazila Livestock Office.

## Broiler chicken — rearing basics
- Cycle ~35 days from day-old chick to ~1.8 kg live weight. Batch commonly 100-1000 birds.
- Brooding: 32-34°C week 1, reduce ~3°C per week. Provide 24h light early, clean water always.
- Feed phases: starter (0-14 d), grower (15-28 d), finisher (29-35 d). ~3.2-3.6 kg feed per bird (FCR ~1.6-1.8).
- Expect 5-8% mortality; keep litter dry to prevent coccidiosis and ammonia.

## Broiler chicken — vaccination schedule
- Day 5: Newcastle Disease (ND) + Infectious Bronchitis (IB), eye/nose drop.
- Day 10-14: Gumboro / Infectious Bursal Disease (IBD) in water, with a booster.
- Day 21: ND booster (Lasota).
- Biosecurity (footbath, all-in/all-out, no visitors) prevents more loss than any vaccine.

## Layer hen — rearing & laying
- Point of lay ~18-20 weeks; a first laying cycle runs ~12 months at ~250-300 eggs/bird.
- Switch to layer ration (with calcium/oyster shell) at ~16 weeks; provide nest boxes.
- Vaccinate ND+IB (wk1), IBD (wk2), ND booster (wk3), Fowl pox (wk5); deworm before lay.
- Cull/sell spent hens at cycle end (salvage value).

## Goat (Black Bengal) — fattening & health
- Fattening cycle ~5-6 months; target ~18 kg. Black Bengal prized for meat & skin quality.
- Feed: grazing + tree leaves + 100-250 g concentrate/day; always provide clean water.
- Vaccinate PPR (Peste des Petits Ruminants) and FMD; Anthrax where endemic. Deworm every 3 months.
- Quarantine new animals 7-10 days. Sell to the Eid/Qurbani market for the best price.

## Beef cattle — fattening
- Fattening cycle ~4-6 months; native/crossbred bulls gain ~0.5-1 kg/day on good ration.
- Ration: green fodder + straw (UMS-treated) + concentrate; avoid steroid/urea misuse.
- Vaccinate FMD, Anthrax, Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) and Black Quarter (BQ). Deworm at start and mid-cycle.
- Time sale to Qurbani/Eid-ul-Adha for peak live-weight prices.

## Dairy cow — lactation & feeding
- Lactation ~305 days; local cows 3-6 L/day, crossbred (HF/Sahiwal) 8-15 L/day.
- Feed for yield: green fodder (Napier), straw, and concentrate ~1 kg per 2-2.5 L milk.
- Re-breed (AI) ~60-90 days after calving to hold a ~12-month calving interval; dry off ~60 days before next calving.
- Vaccinate FMD & HS/BQ; deworm regularly; keep the shed clean and dry to prevent mastitis.

## Common cross-species health notes
- Always quarantine newly bought animals before mixing with the herd/flock.
- Clean water and dry, ventilated housing prevent most disease.
- Keep a treatment/vaccination record per animal or batch.
- For sudden death, bloody diarrhoea, high fever, or abortion, contact a vet immediately — do not self-treat.
