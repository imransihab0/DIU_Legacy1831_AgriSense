# Common Pest and Disease Management — Bangladesh Field Crops
Source: Compiled from DAE plant protection wing bulletins and BRRI/BARI integrated pest management (IPM) guides — publicly available extension material.

## Rice
- Brown planthopper (BPH): hopper-burn patches; risk highest in dense, over-fertilized boro fields with standing water in warm humid weeks. Control: drain field 3-4 days, alternate wetting/drying, avoid excess urea; if >4-5 hoppers/hill spray pymetrozine. Estimated treatment cost ~800-1200 BDT/acre.
- Stem borer: deadhearts (vegetative) or whiteheads (reproductive). Light traps; if >5% deadhearts apply cartap or chlorantraniliprole granules (~900 BDT/acre).
- Rice blast: diamond-shaped lesions; favored by cool nights, high humidity, excess nitrogen. Preventive tricyclazole at panicle initiation in blast-prone areas (~600 BDT/acre). Resistant varieties: BRRI dhan28 is susceptible — prefer BRRI dhan88/89 where blast recurs.
- Bacterial leaf blight: yellowing leaf margins after storms/flooding; no chemical cure — drain field, stop urea, apply MoP.

## Wheat
- Wheat blast (Magnaporthe): bleached spikes; emerged in Bangladesh 2016. Avoid late sowing (after Dec 20 risk rises sharply); seed treatment + tebuconazole at heading in humid warm spells.
- Aphid: spray only if >10 aphids/tiller before grain filling.

## Potato
- Late blight: water-soaked leaf lesions with white mold underside; can destroy a field in 5-7 days of cool foggy weather. Preventive mancozeb every 7-10 days from 40 days after planting; switch to curative (dimethomorph+mancozeb) at first lesion. Season fungicide budget ~4000-8000 BDT/acre.
- Cutworm: seedlings cut at soil line; hand-collect at dusk, chlorpyrifos soil drench if severe.

## Maize
- Fall armyworm: ragged shot-hole leaf feeding, frass in whorl. Scout twice weekly; if >20% plants infested at whorl stage, spray spinetoram or emamectin benzoate into whorls (~1000-1500 BDT/acre). Early sowing (November) escapes peak pressure.

## Mustard
- Aphid: most damaging at flowering; spray malathion in late afternoon (protect pollinators) only if >50 aphids/plant.
- Alternaria blight: dark concentric leaf spots; iprodione or mancozeb at first sign in humid weather.

## Lentil
- Stemphylium blight: main yield killer in humid Novembers; preventive mancozeb at 40 and 55 DAS in dense canopies.

## Onion
- Purple blotch: purple-centered lesions in cloudy humid weather; mancozeb + sticker every 10 days.
- Thrips: silvery streaks in dry weeks; imidacloprid if severe.

## General IPM order of preference
1. Resistant variety and clean seed. 2. Field sanitation and water management. 3. Mechanical (light traps, perching sticks, hand-picking). 4. Biological (Trichogramma cards for borers). 5. Chemical — correct dose, pre-harvest interval respected, never mix >2 products.
